Entrants describe the whole internet in a single word. The entry is that word and nothing else: no files, no code, no links. A judge model compares entries two at a time, weighing how funny the word is, how well it fits the internet as a whole, and how surprising it is, and the top three words share the reward.

Our word is **feral** — the internet, untamed: loud, wild, ungoverned, and impossible to domesticate.

## Run it

```sh
python3 main.py
```
